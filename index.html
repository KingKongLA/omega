Brain and settings

OMEGA's brain is an open-source AI model that downloads once and then runs on this device. Processor brains work on any phone; fast-mode brains use a computer's graphics chip. Nothing you say leaves your device except live-info lookups.

Cloud brain (free)

Online, OMEGA asks a big free AI in the cloud (no key, no signup) for smarter answers. Offline, or if the cloud is busy, the phone brain answers. Want it even smarter? Add free AI keys below, as many as you want (never send keys to anyone): Google Gemini (best: thinks out loud and sees photos), Groq (fastest), OpenRouter (DeepSeek) or Pollinations. ChatGPT (OpenAI) keys work too. OMEGA guesses which kind each key is; if it guesses wrong, tap Edit to fix the name and type. Keys stay on this phone.

Add as many keys as you want. They stay on this phone.

Eyes (photos and videos)

When you attach a photo or video, OMEGA uses a vision brain that runs on this device. It downloads the first time you need it.

Video editing

Attach a video and say what to do: "cut the first 3 seconds", "make it vertical for TikTok" (add "blur the background" to keep the whole picture), "zoom in", "add text DAY 1", "speed it up 2x", "turn it into a GIF", "add captions" (it writes down what is said, TikTok style), "transcribe it", or attach 2–3 clips and say "merge these". Photos too: "make a slideshow video". It all runs on your device; the editor (31 MB) and the captions brain (about 80 MB) download the first time you use them.

Live info sources

Free public sources, no accounts: Open-Meteo (weather), Bing News and NYT (news), DuckDuckGo via Jina Reader and Wikipedia (web), CoinGecko and Coinbase (crypto), Frankfurter and ExchangeRate-API (currency), Pollinations (pictures).
